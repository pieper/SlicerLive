# Kidney — Layer 1: Congenital / developmental variants, pseudotumors, vascular variants

Opened when Layer 0 emits a **count / location / shape / symmetry / contour** violation. A confirmed variant **rewrites the body-plan expectation** for the rest of the run (e.g. horseshoe → expect a midline isthmus; solitary → expect contralateral hypertrophy). Cross-cutting pseudotumor rule: *iso-to-cortex on ALL phases + cortical continuity + no sinus mass effect = variant, not tumor* ([PMC5747680](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5747680/)).

## Congenital / developmental

### Horseshoe kidney
- TRIGGER: two kidneys read as merged / low-lying / midline-crossing tissue; malrotated pelves.
- PROBES: trace parenchyma continuity across midline; isthmus enhancement; ascent level.
- CT SIGNATURE: **isthmus of enhancing (functioning in ~80–90%) parenchyma crossing the midline anterior to the aorta/spine**, joining the lower poles; low-lying (L3–L5, ascent arrested at the IMA); pelves face anteriorly, lower poles point inferomedially; anomalous variable vessels.
- DISTINGUISH: midline **enhancing** bridge **anterior to the great vessels** (vs retroperitoneal nodes, which don't enhance like cortex).
- ACTION: merge both moieties + isthmus into ONE kidney label; expect malrotated collecting systems; map vessels before any surgical inference.
- CITE: [PMC4277381](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4277381/); [NBK431105](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK431105/).

### Crossed fused renal ectopia
- TRIGGER: empty renal fossa on one side + bulky/fused mass on the other.
- CT SIGNATURE: both kidneys same side, fused (crossed moiety usually to the lower pole of the orthotopic kidney); **two distinct hila/collecting systems**.
- DISTINGUISH: the crossed kidney's **ureter still inserts on its ORIGINAL side**, crossing the midline — trace ureters (CT urography).
- ACTION: don't call the empty fossa "nephrectomy/agenesis"; label the fused mass as two kidney moieties.
- CITE: [PMC7226681](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7226681/); [PMC6426565](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6426565/).

### Pelvic / ectopic kidney
- TRIGGER: empty fossa + no ipsilateral renal artery from the aorta.
- CT SIGNATURE: low pelvic/iliac reniform organ, often small/nonreniform, malrotated, anomalous supply from iliac/aorta/sacral/hypogastric (often multiple).
- DISTINGUISH: **corticomedullary enhancement identifies renal tissue** in the pelvis (vs a pelvic tumor/node); empty fossa **without clips**.
- ACTION: extend the search window OUTSIDE the paravertebral fossa; label the pelvic organ as kidney.
- CITE: [NBK563239](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK563239/).

### Renal malrotation
- CT SIGNATURE: kidney **orthotopic** but hilum/pelvis faces anteriorly/anterolaterally (arrested ~90° medial rotation); parenchyma normal; multiple renal arteries in ~20–30%.
- DISTINGUISH: only the **axis** is abnormal (vs ectopia, where the position is abnormal).
- ACTION: don't mistake the anteriorly-rotated pelvis for a hilar mass; adjust the hilum prior.
- CITE: [PMC4770175](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4770175/).

### Duplex / duplicated collecting system
- CT SIGNATURE: two pelvicalyceal systems separated by an **intervening parenchymal band**; elongated kidney; "faceless kidney" on a slice with a sinus but no central system. Weigert–Meyer: **upper-pole ureter inserts ectopically inferomedial** (obstruction/ureterocele); lower-pole orthotopic (reflux). "Drooping lily" of the obstructed upper moiety.
- DISTINGUISH: the bridging tissue **enhances as normal cortex** (like a fused Bertin column) — not a mass; two ureters.
- ACTION: don't split into two organs; don't call the bridge a tumor; an obstructed hydronephrotic upper pole is not a cystic mass.
- CITE: [PMC4770175](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4770175/); [RadiologyKey duplicated system](https://radiologykey.com/duplicated-collecting-system/).

### Unilateral agenesis / solitary kidney + compensatory hypertrophy
- CT SIGNATURE: empty fossa, **no ipsilateral renal artery**, no clips; **"lying-down" flattened adrenal**; fossa filled by colon/small bowel/spleen/pancreatic tail; contralateral **smooth compensatory hypertrophy** (enlarged, normal architecture).
- DISTINGUISH: **absent renal artery + no clips + flat adrenal** = agenesis (vs nephrectomy, which has clips; vs ectopia, which has a kidney elsewhere).
- ACTION: an enlarged solitary kidney is *hypertrophy*, not diffuse disease; the empty fossa is a **fork, never a conclusion**.
- CITE: [RadiologyKey agenesis](https://radiologykey.com/unilateral-renal-agenesis/); [PMID 1404583 (lying-down adrenal)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1404583/).

### Supernumerary kidney
- CT SIGNATURE: a distinct, fully-formed reniform organ with **its own capsule and own independent renal artery** (different vertebral level), separate collecting system that **excretes contrast**, usually caudal to the native kidney.
- DISTINGUISH: independent artery + excreting collecting system (no solid mass excretes contrast).
- ACTION: keep the third kidney; don't discard as duplicate or call it a mass.
- CITE: [PMC12019822](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12019822/).

## Pseudotumors (benign contour/parenchymal mimics — keep as normal parenchyma)

### Hypertrophied column of Bertin
- CT SIGNATURE: infolded septal cortex between medullary pyramids (classically upper/middle-third junction), indenting the sinus but **iso-enhancing to cortex in every phase**, cortical continuity preserved, no necrosis/mass effect.
- DISTINGUISH: tracks cortex on **every** phase. CITE: [PMC12410592](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12410592/).

### Persistent fetal lobulation
- CT SIGNATURE: smooth clefts overlying the **interpyramidal septa (between pyramids), NOT over a calyx**; full-thickness cortex, iso-enhancing.
- DISTINGUISH: a normal pyramid sits within each surface bulge (vs a scar, which overlies a calyx and thins cortex). CITE: [PMC5747680](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5747680/).

### Dromedary (splenic) hump
- CT SIGNATURE: focal cortical bulge on the **lateral border of the mid-LEFT kidney** from splenic molding; iso-enhancing, normal calyces extend into the bulge.
- DISTINGUISH: left-sided, subjacent to spleen, cortex continuous, no mass effect. CITE: [radiogyan dromedary hump](https://radiogyan.com/articles/dromedary-hump-radiology-sign/).

### Prominent renal sinus / sinus lipomatosis
- CT SIGNATURE: **fat-attenuation (~−100 HU)** tissue filling the sinus/hilum, ± fibrous strands; severe → parenchymal atrophy + calculi.
- DISTINGUISH: negative HU (vs soft-tissue pseudotumor/liposarcoma). ACTION: include the fatty sinus in the kidney envelope (don't leave a hole); don't call it an AML. CITE: [PMC2931141](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2931141/).

## Vascular variants (annotate the hilum so downstream reasoning doesn't error)

### Accessory / multiple renal arteries
- CT SIGNATURE (CTA): ≥2 arteries to one kidney (~21% prevalence); extra artery from the aorta in ~88%, terminating hilar/superior-polar/inferior-polar. DISTINGUISH: polar arteries enter the pole directly, not the hilum. CITE: [PMC12358822](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12358822/).

### Retroaortic / circumaortic left renal vein
- CT SIGNATURE: left renal vein passing **behind the aorta** (retroaortic, ~3–9%) or forming a **collar** with anterior + posterior limbs at different levels (circumaortic, ~0.3–3.7%).
- DISTINGUISH: a vessel **with luminal contrast continuous with the IVC**, not para-aortic nodes; watch posterior nutcracker. ACTION: don't label the retroaortic vein as abnormal para-aortic soft tissue. CITE: [PMC12714128](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12714128/); [PMC4003636](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4003636/).
