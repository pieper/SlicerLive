# Kidney — Layer 2: Post-surgical, intervention, and trauma recognizers

Opened by: metallic clips, man-made tubes, a geometric parenchymal cavity, fragmentation/clefts, a perinephric collection, a non-enhancing organ/segment, or an iliac-fossa kidney. Two cross-cutting rules: **keep the organ whole** across clefts/molding via capsule/contour tracing, and after any surgical absence, **scrutinize the fossa for recurrence.**

## Surgical / intervention

### Post radical nephrectomy
- CT SIGNATURE: **absent kidney + empty fossa filled by bowel** (± pancreatic tail/spleen left, liver right); **surgical clips** (Hem-o-Lok ~222 HU) at the vascular pedicle/ureteric stump.
- DISTINGUISH: **clips + empty fossa + organ interposition** = surgical absence (vs agenesis = no clips + flat adrenal; vs ectopia = kidney elsewhere). The empty fossa is a fork, never a conclusion.
- ACTION: label absence; then flag any **arterial-phase hypervascular soft-tissue nodule** in the fossa/psoas margin as suspected recurrence.
- CITE: [PMC3856336](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3856336/); [PMC3340962 (clip HU)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3340962/).

### Partial nephrectomy (nephron-sparing)
- CT SIGNATURE: **wedge/crater-shaped parenchymal defect** at the resected pole ± clips, fluid/blood, gas early; a **bolster ("bolsteroma")** — oval/irregular mass that **shrinks** over serial scans.
- DISTINGUISH: benign postop mass/bolster **decreases** and shows **no nodular enhancement**; growing or nodularly-enhancing tissue at the margin = residual/recurrent tumor (→ Layer 3).
- ACTION: don't read the resection cavity as a cystic/necrotic tumor; expect involution.
- CITE: [PMC2922744](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2922744/); [PMC5036333 (bolster)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5036333/).

### Thermal ablation zone (cryo / RFA)
- CT SIGNATURE: **non-enhancing** low-attenuation defect **larger than the original tumor** (safety margin), often with a **thin, smooth, concentric peripheral rim** of benign reactive enhancement; involutes over time.
- DISTINGUISH: **nodular or crescentic enhancement**, or new/growing enhancing tissue on serial follow-up = residual/recurrent tumor (→ Layer 3). Needs multiphase + prior comparison.
- CITE: [PubMed 19358438](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19358438/); [PubMed 17374856](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17374856/).

### Renal transplant (iliac fossa)
- TRIGGER: reniform organ OUTSIDE the paravertebral fossa.
- CT SIGNATURE: extraperitoneal graft in the **iliac fossa on the iliac vessels**, short pedicle, conspicuous pyramids, mild early pelvicalyceal fullness; native kidneys often small/atrophic/polycystic.
- DISTINGUISH: kidney-shaped organ **low in the pelvis on iliac vessels** (not retroperitoneal). ACTION: label as transplant; then check peri-transplant collections (urinoma/lymphocele/hematoma/abscess).
- CITE: [PMC6544089](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6544089/).

### Nephrostomy / double-J ureteral stent
- CT SIGNATURE: **high-density man-made tube** — nephrostomy entering posterolaterally with a pigtail loop in the pelvis, or a double-J with **proximal coil in the pelvis + distal coil in the bladder**; expect streak artifact.
- ACTION: recognize as hardware (not calculus/tumor); verify both coils seated. CITE: [radiologykey nephrostomy/stent](https://radiologykey.com/percutaneous-nephrostomy-and-antegrade-ureteral-stenting/).

### Renal atrophy (post-intervention / chronic obstruction)
- CT SIGNATURE: **small kidney, diffuse cortical thinning**, diminished/delayed nephrogram; chronic obstruction → thin rim around a dilated system; reflux → focal polar scars over clubbed calyces.
- DISTINGUISH: **global smooth volume loss with thin intact cortex** ± contralateral hypertrophy. ACTION: don't drop the small poorly-enhancing kidney below threshold. CITE: [radiologykey diffuse renal](https://radiologykey.com/diffuse-renal-parenchymal-disorders/).

## Trauma (multiphase CT; AAST grading)

### Laceration
- CT SIGNATURE: **hypoattenuating linear/wedge cleft** through the enhancing nephrogram (best CMP/NP); AAST II <1 cm superficial, III >1 cm deep, **IV if it reaches the collecting system** (urine leak on excretory phase).
- ACTION: keep the fragmented kidney as **one organ** via capsule tracing; use delayed phase to separate laceration (no contrast in cleft) from collecting-system injury (extravasation). CITE: [PMC3908505](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3908505/).

### Subcapsular vs perinephric hematoma
- CT SIGNATURE: **subcapsular** = crescent that **flattens/indents the cortex** (can cause Page kidney); **perinephric** = collection in Gerota's space that does **NOT** deform the contour; acute blood ~40–90 HU, non-enhancing.
- DISTINGUISH (the discriminator): **contour deformity = subcapsular; no mass effect = perinephric.** ACTION: use unenhanced/comparison HU — high-HU clot is not enhancement. CITE: [RadioGraphics 21:3 subcapsular](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/radiographics.21.3.g01ma11557).

### Active arterial extravasation vs pseudoaneurysm
- CT SIGNATURE: focal pooling **within ~10 HU of the aorta** on arterial phase, within a hematoma, that **enlarges/persists on delayed phase** (active bleed).
- DISTINGUISH: a **round, well-defined focus that FADES and doesn't enlarge** on delayed = contained pseudoaneurysm. ACTION: don't call an arterial-matching focus a hyperenhancing tumor nidus. CITE: [AJR 178:1](https://ajronline.org/doi/10.2214/ajr.178.1.1780017).

### Shattered / devascularized kidney (AAST V)
- CT SIGNATURE: multiple fragments with **large non-enhancing devascularized segments**; **global absence of nephrogram** + medial perihilar hematoma if the pedicle is avulsed/thrombosed; delayed cortical rim sign may appear.
- DISTINGUISH: reniform **outline preserved but no/patchy enhancement**; check the renal artery for occlusion/avulsion. ACTION: a non-enhancing kidney is a **whole-organ false-negative trap** — keep it. CITE: [RadioGraphics 29:7](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/abs/10.1148/rg.297095071).

### Urinoma
- CT SIGNATURE: well-circumscribed **water-attenuation** perirenal collection that **fills with excreted contrast on the delayed/excretory phase** — the defining sign.
- DISTINGUISH: delayed opacification (vs hematoma, which never opacifies; vs cyst, which never opacifies). CITE: [PMC (RadioGraphics urinoma)](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/abs/10.1148/rg.235035029).
