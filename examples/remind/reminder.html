The surgical timeline — how many of the … cases reach each stage

Every patient has exactly two studies. The one containing ultrasound is the intra-operative study; the other is the pre-operative MRI. Within the operation the stage is named by the series itself (US_pre_dura, US_post_dura, US_pre_imri). Bar colour encodes modality — blue for MR, orange for ultrasound — and gets darker as the operation progresses.

Per-case coverage — click any row to open its timeline

One row per case; a filled cell means that acquisition (or that segmented structure) exists in IDC for that case. Sorted by coverage, so the incomplete cases sit at the top. The row also carries the numbers — series count and total size — so nothing here is readable only by colour.

Segmented structures

Bar colour is the colour the viewer paints that structure with, in 2D fill and 3D shell alike. tumor_residual is annotated on the intra-operative MRI — pairing it with the pre-operative tumor label is what makes extent of resection legible.
