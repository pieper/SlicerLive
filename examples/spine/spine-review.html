121 cancer patients (55 spine metastases with expert labels, 66 multiple myeloma with nnU-Net labels). One line per case. Each axis is a vertebral level; the value is 1 − Dice between the reference label and the SPINEPS label of the same name — 0 means perfect agreement, 1 means no overlap. Levels absent from the reference are simply not drawn, so a line covers only the vertebrae that were annotated.

A flat line along the bottom is a case where SPINEPS and the reference agree at every annotated level. A step up to ~1.0 that persists downward is the signature of an off-by-one label shift — the two segmentations describe the same bone under different names. Isolated spikes are usually a single mis-segmented vertebra. Drag on any axis to filter; hover a line to preview, click to open the interactive SlicerLive comparison.
