Fiber Tracts — capsule tubes + lava lamp WebGPU

About 1,500 synthetic streamlines shaped like SlicerDMRI fiber bundles (a helix, a U-arc, a fan and a diagonal) are drawn as chains of capsules, which makes piecewise-linear tubes. Each tube is hit exactly inside the same ray-march as an animated volume of three breathing glow blobs and the markup fiducials, so everything overlaps in the right depth order. Ported from the SlicerWGPU "lava lamp" field-compositing test. Drag the yellow attractor and the blobs chase it. Drag empty space to rotate. The SlicerLive badge sets each bundle's opacity.  ·  SlicerLive
